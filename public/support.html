The Plug Worship Support

The Plug Worship is a private app intended for approved members of The Plug organization. If you need help accessing or using the app, please contact support.

Support Email

Email support at: support@theplugworship.com

Help Topics

  • Login access
  • Account issues
  • Technical problems
  • Membership payment tracking
  • General app questions

Account Deletion

Members can delete their account inside the app through: Profile or Settings > Delete Account.

The app will ask for confirmation before completing account deletion.

App Access

Access to The Plug Worship is limited to approved members of The Plug organization. If you believe you should have access but cannot sign in, contact support with your name and the email address used for your account.
