The Plug Worship Support

The Plug Worship is a private app intended for approved members of The Plug organization. If you need help accessing or using the app, please contact support.

Contact Support

For assistance with The Plug Worship, email: support@theplugworship.com

Email Support

Help Topics

Support is available for the following app-related topics:

  • Login access
  • Account issues
  • Technical problems
  • Membership payment tracking
  • General app questions

Account Deletion

Members can delete their account inside the app through:

Profile or Settings > Delete Account

The app will ask for confirmation before completing account deletion.

App Access

The Plug Worship is only available to approved members of The Plug organization. If you believe you should have access but cannot sign in, contact support with your name and the email address you used to create your account.
